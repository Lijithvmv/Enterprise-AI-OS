# Contributing

Contributions are welcome. This list stays useful only if every entry earns its place.

## Guidelines
- **One line per entry, explaining *why it's here*** — the value, not just the name. Curation over volume.
- **Put it in the right section**, alphabetical-ish within a section is fine; quality-ordered is better.
- **Real, maintained resources only** — no dead links, no self-promotion without substance, no paywalled-only content unless it's genuinely worth it.
- **Neutral, accurate descriptions** — no marketing language.
- Keep formatting consistent: `- [Name](url) — one-line why.`

## How
1. Fork and create a branch.
2. Add your entry (or fix a link) and check it renders.
3. Open a PR with a short note on why it belongs.

By contributing you agree your contribution is licensed under this repository's license.
